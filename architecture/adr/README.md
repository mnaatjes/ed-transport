# Architectural Decision Records Governance and Index

Governed by Markdown Architectural Decision Records (MADR 3.0).

---

## 1. Principles & Quality Gates

1. **Immutable Historical Snapshot:** Once accepted and committed, an ADR's decision text is never rewritten or deleted.
2. **Supersedence Rule:** When requirements or architectural direction changes, author a new ADR that explicitly supersedes the prior record.
3. **Traceability:** Every major architectural decision must link to relevant upstream use cases or risk items.
