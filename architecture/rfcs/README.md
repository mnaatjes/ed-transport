# Collaborative Engineering RFCs Governance and Index

Governed by the Unified Process Inception Discipline for collaborative architectural evaluation.

---

## 1. Principles & Quality Gates

1. **Objective Trade-Off Matrix:** Every RFC evaluates at least two viable alternative solutions against explicit decision drivers.
2. **Parent/Child Proposal Hierarchy:** Complex, cross-cutting proposals are grouped into parent directories (`NNNN_proposal_name/`) with child specifications (`NNNNa_child_spec.md`).
3. **Consensus Outcome:** Accepted RFCs transition into binding ADRs in `architecture/adr/`.
